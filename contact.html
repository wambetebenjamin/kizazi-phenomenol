A Christian youth ministry and fellowship serving East Africa — Kenya • Uganda • Tanzania • Rwanda “Kizazi” means generation. We exist so this generation knows Jesus deeply, worships boldly, and gets sent out into campuses, careers and culture.

Students, fresh graduates, young professionals — anyone who’s in it. If you’re in East Africa you can join cells, events and trips; if you’re anywhere else, Fridays are online, so the whole world can catch up with us.
