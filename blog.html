Devotional 12 Aug 2026 CM Media & Creative Crew

What it means to be a “phenomenal” generation

“Let no one despise you for your youth” — that’s not a permission slip, it’s a commission. Paul isn’t telling Timothy he’s too young; he’s telling him the world will be looking for a reason to dismiss him, and the best answer is a life.

A phenomenal generation isn’t louder or flashier than the one before it. It’s a generation that sets the example — in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity. The example, not the argument, is what gets people asking questions.

So this week, don’t defend your faith — display it. Let your cell, your campus and your timeline make the claim. The generation on fire is the generation that shows up first.

Practical 5 Sep 2026 DC Discipleship Cells

Staying rooted between the Fridays: 5 habits

Fridays are the heartbeat, but faith lives in the four days between. Here are the five habits we keep preaching in the cells — because consistency is the culture.

1) Five verses before five minutes of scrolling. 2) One prayer you can actually finish saying out loud. 3) One person in your cell you check on before the weekend. 4) One honest question you’re not afraid to bring next Friday. 5) One act of care no one asked for — sent, done, unposted.

None of these are big. That’s the point. Rooted doesn’t grow by leaps — it grows by never missing.
