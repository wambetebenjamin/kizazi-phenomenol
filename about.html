We are Ministers’ Kids

KIZAZI (“generation”) Phenomenal is a Christian youth fellowship serving Kenya • Uganda • Tanzania • Rwanda. We are on a mission to grow stronger, connect deeper, and shine brighter as Ministers' Kids

We gather online every Friday, meet in cells through the week, and cross borders for conferences, campus tours and serve trips. Worship that moves, the Word taught plainly, and a family that carries you — that’s the rhythm.

It all stands on one commission: “Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the example for the believers.” · 1 Timothy 4:12
