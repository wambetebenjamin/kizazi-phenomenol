Who we are

We're not a club.
We're a generation.

“Kizazi” is Swahili for generation. We believe this generation isn't the “church of tomorrow” — we're the church of right now. KIZAZI Phenomenal exists to help young people across East Africa meet Jesus, grow deep roots, find their purpose and light up their schools, campuses, workplaces and cities.

What we do

Eight ways we move

Every ministry is a doorway. Pick one, jump in, and watch God use you.

Worship & The Word

Loud, honest, Spirit-led worship and Bible teaching that answers real questions — not just religious slogans.

Discipleship Cells

Small weekly groups in towns and on campuses where we do life together, study the Word and keep each other accountable.

Prayer & Intercession

A house of prayer for a generation. We contend for our families, cities and nations — and we expect answers.

Friday Online Catch-Up

Our weekly digital family night. Word, worship, chats and check-ins from wherever you are in East Africa.

Outreach & Missions

Street evangelism, school visits, community care and cross-border missions. Faith with hands and feet.

Creative & Media Lab

Music, film, design, spoken word and content. We raise creators who tell God's story beautifully.

Mentorship & Career

Older-and-wiser believers walking with you through school, work, business and life decisions.

Community & Care

Nobody walks alone. Practical support, counselling referrals and a family that shows up.
