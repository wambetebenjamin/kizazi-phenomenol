About Us

We Are The Generation That Sets The Example

“Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.” — 1 Timothy 4:12. KIZAZI Phenomenal is a family of students and young workers across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda, gathered every Friday online and in cells through the week.
